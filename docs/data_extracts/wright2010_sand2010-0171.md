## § 2.3 — Loop operating conditions near the critical point

### Table 2.1 (p.23) — three-state operating-point summary  [Confidence A]

- **Source location:** [Wright2010_SAND2010_0171, Table 2.1, p.23]
- **Content:** Inlet/outlet T, P, h, ṁ, η, RPM at three operating points
  (Design Point: 305.3 K / 7690 kPa; Vapor Side: 295 K / 5000 kPa;
  Liquid Side: 295 K / 7690 kPa). Design pressure ratio 1.818, η ≈ 0.664,
  ṁ = 3.53 kg/s @ 75 000 rpm.
- **Used in this repo by:** `validation/experimental_data/SNL_compressor_data.csv`
  rows tagged `Wright2010_SAND_T2.1_{design,vapor,liquid}`.
- **Used as:** verbatim rows for `validate_against_sandia.py`.

### §2.3 narrative (p.20) — pseudo-critical density change  [Confidence B]

- **Source location:** [Wright2010_SAND2010_0171, §2.3, p.20]
- **Content:** "even though the pressure ratio is 1.8, the density changes
  only by 10 % (from 0.608 kg/liter to 0.670 kg/liter)" along the
  compression process near the pseudo-critical line. Heat capacity
  reportedly increases by a factor of 10–30 depending on pressure.
- **Used in this repo by:** `SNL_compressor_data.csv` row
  `Wright2010_SAND_S2.3_pseudocrit` (608 kg/m³ at 305.3 K, 7.69 MPa).
  CoolProp returns 587 kg/m³ — 3.4 % low, consistent with the prose's
  one-decimal precision.
- **Used as:** B-grade density anchor for CoolProp regression.

### §2.3 narrative (p.21) — control-system reality check  [Confidence D]

- **Source location:** [Wright2010_SAND2010_0171, §2.3, p.21]
- **Content:** "very large amount of heat transfer is required to change
  the temperature, even by 1 K"; RTDs have ~0.2 K accuracy; gas-chiller
  ΔT only 0.7 K when rejecting 50.2 kW.
- **Used in this repo by:** referenced in `docs/02_phase2_cfd_rom.md`
  prologue (TODO: add expectation-management section pointing here).
- **Used as:** qualitative argument for tight CFD-side instrumentation
  expectations and ROM-side error bars.

## § 3 — Turbomachinery and test-loop hardware

- **Source location:** [Wright2010_SAND2010_0171, Table 3.1, p.25]
- **Content:** 50 kWe motor / 120–150 kWe alternator; design 75 000 rpm,
  max 80 000 rpm; housing MAWP 2600 psia; rotor cavity ~150–200 psia;
  ṁ = 3.53 kg/s; pressure ratio 1.8; Sm-Co PM with Inconel cladding;
  uncanned stator; η ≈ 66 % total-to-static.
- **Used in this repo by:** *not yet*. Candidate for `cases/case03/` real-
  scale geometry, and for Modelica `TurboCompressor` default parameters.
- **Used as:** verbatim design-point parameters once integrated.

  back-swept blade angle β₂b = −50°; 12 blades; blade thickness 0.762 mm;

- **Used in this repo by:** *not yet*. Strong candidate for replacing

## § 5 — Test results

### Figure 5-11 caption (p.57) — performance-map sweep envelope  [Confidence B]

- **Source location:** [Wright2010_SAND2010_0171, Figure 5-11, p.57]
- **Content:** Compressor performance map taken at inlet 304.1 K /
  7.377 MPa; sweep over the test sequence covered 304.3–307 K and
  7.700–8.139 MPa. Map shows corrected enthalpy rise vs. corrected mass
  flow at 45/50/55/60/63/64/65/67/80/90 krpm.
- **Used in this repo by:** `SNL_compressor_data.csv` row
  `Wright2010_SAND_F5.11_perfmap`.
- **Used as:** envelope of compressor inlet conditions for any
  surrogate model trained against SNL data.

### §5.3.1 narrative (p.54–55) — CBC_081202_1003 transient  [Confidence B]

- **Source location:** [Wright2010_SAND2010_0171, §5.3.1, p.54–55]
- **Content:** Spin test at 55 krpm; inlet 304.4 K / 7.536 MPa, outlet
  pressure ramp 1.10 → 1.76 MPa over the speed steps; valve-closure
  sequence at 12 335 s reduced ṁ from 3.95 kg/s (8.7 lb/s) to 1.64 kg/s
  (3.6 lb/s). Outlet pressure peak 12.1 MPa.
- **Used in this repo by:** `SNL_compressor_data.csv` row
  `Wright2010_SAND_S5.3.1_CBC081202`.
- **Used as:** transient benchmark anchor for future Modelica
  dynamic-simulation tests.

### Figure 5-13 (p.60) — labelled inlet/outlet diagram  [Confidence B]

- **Source location:** [Wright2010_SAND2010_0171, Figure 5-13, p.60]
- **Content:** Cutaway labels "Compressor Inlet 305 K, 7690 kPa" and
  "Compressor Exit Chamber 323 K, 13840 kPa" — the rounded design
  point.
- **Used in this repo by:** `SNL_compressor_data.csv` row
  `Wright2010_SAND_F5.13_design` (rounded sanity reference).

### §5.4 (p.59) — Vrancik windage formula  [Confidence A]

- **Source location:** [Wright2010_SAND2010_0171, §5.4, p.59]
- **Content:** Pwr = π · C_d(Re) · ρ · r⁴ · ω³ · L_r;
  Re = r · t_gap · ω · ρ / μ; C_d = 2.04 + 1.768·ln(Re·√C_d) − 1/√C_d.
  Discharge coefficient varies 0.01–0.06 over Re = 10⁴–10⁸.
- **Used in this repo by:** *not yet*. Strong candidate for a physical
  soft constraint in `rom/` training (penalise predictions that violate
  the ρ-first-order, ω-third-order scaling).
- **Used as:** sanity-check formula and ROM regulariser candidate.
- **Indirect citation:** [Vrancik1968_NASA_TN_D4849].

### §5.5 (p.65) — Egli labyrinth-seal leakage correlation  [Confidence A]

- **Source location:** [Wright2010_SAND2010_0171, §5.5 / Table 5.3, p.65]
- **Content:** Egli correlation parameters reported by Barber-Nichols
  for the four-tooth labyrinth seal; leakage 1–2 % of mass flow per seal
  at design inlet pressure.
- **Used in this repo by:** `modelica/AdvancedReactor_sCO2_Library/Components/Turbomachinery/LabyrinthSeal.mo`
  (default `N_teeth = 4`, `r_seal ≈ 9.4 mm`, `t_clearance = 0.254 mm`,
  `alpha_egli = 0.65`).
- **Used as:** verbatim correlation; defaults seed the BYOD chart
  interface (industrial users override via `chartFileName`).

### §5.4 narrative (p.58) — windage scale  [Confidence B]

- **Source location:** [Wright2010_SAND2010_0171, §5.4, p.58]
- **Content:** Windage at design conditions estimated 3–4 kW (significant
  fraction of motor input ~50 kW); rotor-cavity pressure must be reduced
  to <300 psia by Haskel pumps to keep losses manageable.
- **Used in this repo by:** *not yet*.
- **Used as:** an order-of-magnitude check on any rotor-loss model.

### Figure 5-12 (p.58) — measured vs. modelled efficiency  [Confidence C]

- **Source location:** [Wright2010_SAND2010_0171, Figure 5-12, p.58]
- **Content:** Measured t-s efficiency at 45/50/55/56/60/64 krpm peaks
  at ~0.67 around 4–5 lb/s corrected mass flow, falling to ~0.4–0.5 at
  the surge or choke ends of the curves. Comparison with BNI's mean-line
  model.
- **Used in this repo by:** *not yet*.
- **Used as:** qualitative envelope for any surrogate-model error
  budget; not transcribed as a hard CI gate.
