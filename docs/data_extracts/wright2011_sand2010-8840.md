## § 1.2 — Fluid density ratio at the heat-rejection unit  [Confidence A]

- **Source location:** [Wright2011_SAND2010_8840, §1.2, p.13–14]
- **Content:** at the waste-heat-rejection condition (P > 6 MPa, ~900 psia),
  liquid CO₂ density is **0.75 kg/L** and vapor CO₂ density is
  **0.21 kg/L** — a liquid:vapor ratio of only ~3:1 (vs. 1000:1 for
  steam). This small ratio is the physical reason the SNL radial
  compressor can "pump" liquid CO₂ even though it was designed for
  vapor-phase sCO₂ near the critical point.
- **Used in this repo by:** *not yet*. Strong candidate for a sanity
  check in `src/sco2_property_explorer.py` and a verification target
  for any condensing-mode extension to `src/sco2_cycle.py`.
- **Used as:** anchor for any "low compression-work" claim about
  condensing sCO₂ cycles.

## § 2.1 — Condensing power cycle, T > 450 °C  [Confidence B]

- **Source location:** [Wright2011_SAND2010_8840, §2.1, p.16–20]
- **Content:** lowering compressor inlet T from 305 K → 300 K gives
  +1.5 percentage-point cycle efficiency; 305 K → 295 K gives +2.7 pp.
  Figure 2-4 shows condensing-Brayton cycle efficiency 46.6 % @ 302 K
  rising to 48.8 % @ 293 K (peak P = 20 MPa).
- **Used in this repo by:** *not yet*. Candidate for a "condensing-mode
  extension" example notebook in the Jupyter Book.
- **Used as:** order-of-magnitude check for any condensing-cycle
  optimisation routine.

## § 5 — Compression-loop test results  [Confidence A]

- **Source location:** [Wright2011_SAND2010_8840, §5, p.30–41]
- **Content:** three test campaigns —
  (1) compressor pumping liquid CO₂ near the critical point (Fig 5-1,
  T-s diagram with stations 1, 2, 3 sketched);
  (2) constant-pressure T-D curves showing density behaviour near the
  critical point (Fig 5-4 at 1060 psia ≈ 7.31 MPa; Fig 5-5
  multi-pressure overlay);
  (3) condensing experiments with the modified loop (Fig 5-7 sub-panels:
  supercritical 305 K inlet; liquid 303 K inlet; liquid 296 K inlet;
  Fig 5-8 two-phase 296 K inlet).
- **Used in this repo by:** *not yet*. Candidate for ROM training data
  (Fig 5-3 compressor map at 35/40/45 krpm spinning on liquid CO₂) and
  for a `case04_condensing` validation case once Phase 2 micro-channel
  pipeline is stable.
- **Used as:** measurement-grade anchor for condensing-mode model
  validation.
